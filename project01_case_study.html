THE PROBLEM

Every month, real estate agents export their lead lists from Zillow, Realtor.com, and Facebook Ads — and every month, those exports are unusable without hours of manual cleanup. Phone numbers arrive in five different formats. The same contact appears multiple times. Names are in ALL CAPS. Budgets say "$450k" in one row and "650000" in the next. And leads with a valid phone number but no email sit quietly in the file, doing nothing — potential commissions that never get followed up.

SAME-NAME CONFLICT SCORING

Detects same-name groups and scores each pair using business logic — area codes, budgets, sources, and FL metro geography — not fuzzy string matching.

Verdict Scoring signals
🔴 LIKELY DUPLICATE Same area code (+3) + one missing email (+2). Score ≥ 4.
🟡 POSSIBLE DUPLICATE Same source, similar budget, partial overlap. Score 1–3. Agent decides.
🟢 PROBABLY DIFFERENT Different FL metros (Tampa vs Jacksonville = −2). Score ≤ 0. Keep both.
